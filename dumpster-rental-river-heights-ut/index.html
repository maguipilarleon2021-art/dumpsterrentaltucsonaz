Frequently Asked Questions

1. How quickly can I get a dumpster delivered?

In most areas of the United States, we offer same-day or next-day delivery depending on container availability and your location. When you contact us, our dispatch team will confirm the earliest available delivery window for your zip code.

2. What size dumpster do I need for my project?

The right size depends on the volume and type of waste you're generating. A 10-yard container works well for small cleanouts and minor demo work, while a 20-yard is our most popular size for home renovations and medium-sized cleanouts. For larger jobs, a 30- or 40-yard container suits full construction sites or complete home cleanouts. Our team is happy to help you choose — just describe your project and we'll guide you to the right fit.

3. Are there items I'm not allowed to put in the dumpster?

Yes. Prohibited items typically include hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, motor oil, asbestos-containing materials, batteries, tires, and certain electronics. We'll walk you through exactly what's allowed when you book your container so there are no surprises at pickup. If you're ever unsure about a specific item, just ask us before you toss it in.

4. How is pricing calculated and are there extra fees?

Our pricing is based on container size, rental duration, and your location. Delivery, pickup, and a standard weight allowance are all included in the base price. If your load exceeds the weight limit, an overage fee applies — but we'll disclose that threshold upfront so you can plan accordingly. There are no mystery charges, ever.

5. Can I keep the dumpster longer than my original rental period?

Absolutely. If you need more time, just give us a call before your rental period ends and we'll extend it for an additional daily or weekly rate. We work with customers to accommodate project delays and unexpected timeline changes whenever possible.
